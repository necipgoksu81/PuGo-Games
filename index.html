Our first game

Zing Blast!

A block puzzle with a juicy twist. Swipe colorful blocks onto the board, match three to blast them and sort every juice into the right cup. Every swipe moves a whole line, so one smart move can set off a chain of blasts.

What's inside

Easy to learn, fun to master

Everything listed here is in the game today.

Slide from any side

Swipe a block in from any edge. The whole line slides with it, so every move changes the board.

Blasts and combos

Match three to blast. One clever move can set off a whole chain of blasts and combos.

Three boosters

Shuffle the board, drop a Bomb or Paint away a whole color when a level gets tricky.

Fresh twists

Chained cups, cages, locked blocks, stone blocks, keys, locked bottles and boards of many shapes.

Race the clock

Take your time on the easy levels, or race the clock and beat the challenge levels.

Nothing in the way

No sign-up and no chat. Just open the game and start sliding.

Your own juice stand

Every level fills a cup

Every level you win puts a fresh cup of juice on your stand. Sell your cups to earn coins, then spend them on boosters, extra waiting places, extra time and lives. Say hello to the cheerful juice girl too: tap her, the fruit bowls, the register and the bottles to see what happens!

The studio

How we build

PuGo Games is an independent studio. We build cheerful puzzle games and spend our time on how they feel in the hand — the pop of a blast, the flow of the juice, the moment a chain resolves.

  • 1
    Depth over breadth We would rather ship one mechanic that holds up over hundreds of runs than several that do not.
  • 2
    Global from day one Every title launches worldwide, localised for each market it reaches.
  • 3
    Fair by design Free to play and ad-supported, with no pressure to spend.
