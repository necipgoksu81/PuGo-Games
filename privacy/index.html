Privacy Policy

All PuGo Games titles — Effective / Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PuGo Games ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses information in connection with our mobile games, including Zing Blast!, Blade Drop, and any other game we publish under the PuGo Games name (each a "Game", together the "Games"), and our website pugogames.com. The Games are available on Google Play and other app stores. By downloading, installing, or playing a Game, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Games.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

The Games do not require account registration or login. We do not directly collect your name, postal address, phone number, email address, or precise location. The Games do not include chat, messaging, or any user-generated content shared with other players.

1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To let you keep your progress, each Game stores certain information locally on your device only (using standard device storage). This data is not transmitted to our servers — we do not operate a server that receives it. Depending on the Game, it includes:

  • Scores, level or depth progress, and best results
  • In-game currency, upgrades, unlocked items, and shop or idle-building progress
  • Daily streaks, daily puzzle progress, and reward or milestone progress
  • Timers, cooldowns, and offline-earnings calculations
  • Game settings (sound, music, vibration, language)
  • Tutorial completion status

This data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party Services

The Games use third-party software development kits ("SDKs") that may collect certain information automatically to provide advertising, measure performance, attribute installs, and improve the Games. This may include:

CategoryExamples
Device identifiersAdvertising ID (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS), device model, operating system version, app version, language
Network informationIP address (used to derive approximate, non-precise location such as country/region)
App interaction / gameplay dataSession start/end times and duration, dates and times of play, level or progress reached, progress and completion events, points where a session was exited or abandoned, ad views/clicks, purchase events, crash and diagnostic logs
Install attribution dataWhich advertising campaign or source led to an install, and in-game events linked to it
Approximate locationDerived only from IP address or device settings; we do not request GPS/precise location permission

This information is collected and processed by the third-party providers listed in Section 2, according to their own privacy policies, and may be used to serve and personalize advertising, measure advertising campaigns, prevent fraud, provide aggregate analytics, and understand how players engage with a Game (for example, which levels are difficult or where players tend to stop playing).

Publishing / distribution partners. A Game may be published, promoted, or distributed in partnership with a third-party publishing partner. In that case the partner's own analytics, attribution, and/or mediation SDKs are integrated into the Game and may independently collect information of the types described in this Section 1.3, under that partner's own privacy policy. The partners we currently work with are listed in Section 2.

1.4 In-App Purchases

If a Game offers in-app purchases (for example, an option to remove ads), all payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing (or the Apple App Store on iOS). We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or other payment credentials — this information is processed directly by the store under its own privacy policy.

1.5 Website

Our website pugogames.com is a static information site. It does not require registration and does not use advertising cookies of its own. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, which we use only to respond to you.

Other advertising networks connected through the mediation platform of our publishing partner. The list of ad networks can change over time and is available through the publishing partner's privacy policy and, where provided, in the Game's privacy settings.

We may add, remove, or change third-party providers over time. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Games
  • To display advertising and measure ad performance and campaign results
  • To personalize advertising where you have provided consent (see Section 4)
  • To diagnose technical issues and crashes
  • To understand aggregate, anonymized usage trends (e.g., which levels are popular)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, or abuse
  • To respond to your requests and to comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some jurisdictions (e.g., California) define certain advertising-related data sharing as a "sale" or "share" — see Section 6 for your choices regarding this.

4. Advertising & Your Choices

The Games are supported by advertising. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalized (based on your advertising ID and interests) or non-personalized.

4.1 Users in the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland

If you are located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a Game will present a consent message (for example via Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized ads, in accordance with the GDPR and applicable ePrivacy rules. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time from the Game's Settings → Privacy Choices menu, where available.

4.2 Managing Ad Personalization on Your Device

You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your advertising identifier at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (or "Delete advertising ID" on newer Android versions)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"

You can also review Google's own ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

5. Children's Privacy

The Games are general-audience games and are not intentionally directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that violates applicable law, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or the EU/UK GDPR's provisions on children's data.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us at the address in Section 10 so we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete such information.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Because the Games do not maintain user accounts or a central database of personal profiles, most of the data described in Section 1.3 is held and controlled by our third-party providers rather than by us directly. Depending on your location (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK, or U.S. state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the following rights, which you can generally exercise through the device controls in Section 4.2, directly with the relevant provider, or by following the steps on our Data Deletion page:

  • Access, correction, deletion, or portability of data associated with your advertising identifier (via your device settings, the provider's tools, or by contacting us)
  • Objection to or restriction of processing for advertising purposes
  • Right to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising (California CCPA/CPRA, and similar U.S. state laws)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for EEA/UK residents)

For requests that require our direct involvement, contact us using the details in Section 10. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored game data (Section 1.2) remains on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data collected by third-party providers (Section 1.3) is retained according to their own retention policies, as described in their respective privacy policies linked in Section 2.

8. Data Security

We rely on the security measures implemented by Google Play, our publishing and advertising partners, and Unity Technologies for any data their SDKs process. While we and our partners take reasonable steps to protect information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers may process and store information on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States, subject to appropriate safeguards required by applicable law (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).
