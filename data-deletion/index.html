Data Deletion Instructions

All PuGo Games titles — Effective / Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data associated with our games, including Zing Blast! and Blade Drop. Our games do not require an account, a Facebook login, or any personal profile, so we usually do not hold data that identifies you directly. See our Privacy Policy for what is collected and why.

1. Delete data stored on your device

  1. Uninstall the game from your device, or
  2. On Android: Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data.

This removes your progress, settings, and any information saved locally by the game.

2. Request deletion of data linked to your device

Analytics, attribution, and advertising providers may hold data linked to your device's advertising ID. To ask us to request deletion of that data:

  1. Send an email to support@pugogames.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Include the name of the game and, if possible, your device's advertising ID (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) so we can identify the data.
  3. We will confirm your request and process it within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires. Where a partner holds the data, we will forward the request to that partner.

3. Reset or limit advertising data yourself

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off tracking for the app.

4. Data held by other providers

Some services act independently, for example Google, Meta (Facebook), and advertising networks. You can manage or delete your data directly with them through their privacy tools. Links to their privacy policies are listed in Section 2 of our Privacy Policy.
