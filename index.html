What's inside

One board, endless ways to clear it

Everything below ships in the current build — no roadmap promises, no placeholders.

Slide from any edge

Most block games drop pieces from one direction. Here every swipe repacks the entire board toward the opposite wall.

Diagonals count

Rows and columns are only the start. Three in a diagonal blasts too — and it changes how you read the whole grid.

Explosive combo chains

Every clear repacks the board, which can trigger the next match. Keep the chain alive and the multiplier climbs to UNSTOPPABLE.

Three power-ups

Bomb clears a 3×3 area, Shuffle rebuilds the layout from scratch, and Paint turns every block of one colour into another.

A new puzzle daily

Daily Puzzle gives you a hand-set board with a limited number of blocks and a guaranteed solution. Solve it, keep your streak.

No pressure, no nonsense

No sign-up, no email, no chat, no countdown timers working against you. It plays offline and it respects your data.
